## Behaviour

- [x] Program changes: all ten programs were selected in turn, 32 intervals
      spanned a change or a freeze transition, and none overloaded or faulted.
      Whether a change is free of clicks is a listening judgement and is not
      established by the capture.
- [ ] Every live `SetParameter` control moves the sound without a zipper or a
      click — the pots were swept during the capture (`updates=` reaches 320 in
      one interval) with no overload, but freedom from zipper noise is a
      listening judgement
- [x] Freeze holds the tail, and releases it cleanly — exercised on all ten
      programs, 118 frozen intervals, no fault. The wet level steps at the gate
      by a program-dependent amount; this is characterised in TECHNICAL.md and
      is not a defect.
- [x] Normal operation: no staging failures or MDMA errors
- [ ] Injected staging fault — not exercised
- [ ] Deliberate overload reduces the line count and recovers — not exercised.
      The heaviest program peaked at 81.7%, 8 points under the 90% guard, so
      the recovery path was never entered.
- [ ] Thirty minutes on the heaviest program — not exercised. Each program was
      held for 20 to 34 seconds; the whole capture is about five minutes.
- [x] `staging ... failures=` **0** , `mdma_errors=` **0** , `mdma_status=0x0`

## Not established by this capture

The delay-memory test, cold-boot repetition, the injected staging fault, the
deliberate overload and the thirty-minute soak. The first four are short; the
soak is the one that would take real time. None of them is contradicted by the
capture — they are simply not evidenced by it.
